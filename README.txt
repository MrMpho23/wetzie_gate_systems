WETZIE_GATE SYSTEMS WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: page structure and website content
- style.css: colours, layout, responsive design and styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, current year and WhatsApp enquiry form

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this folder.
2. Keep all three files in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome or Edge.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open script.js and replace:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "27REPLACEWITHNUMBER";
   with your WhatsApp number in international format, digits only.
   Example format: 27 followed by 9 digits. Do not include + or spaces.
2. Replace the sample email and phone details in index.html.
3. Add your real company logo and confirm that the services listed are services you offer.
4. The hero background image loads from Unsplash and requires an internet connection.
5. The contact form prepares a WhatsApp message; it does not send an email or store enquiries.

This is a starter website. It is not published online until you purchase/connect a domain and hosting service.
